# Candidates — Funniest One-Line Out-of-Office Reply

Gate (from kit): entry.txt must be UTF-8, 1–160 characters, non-blank, one line.
Judge: pairwise, laugh lands on first read > freshest joke > still reads like a
real away message a person would switch on. Avoid the tired beach / inbox /
"limited access to email" lines.

## Candidates

1. I'm out of office. My emails are being handled by Future Me, who has already decided to ignore most of them.
2. I'm on leave until Monday, at which point I will return to ignoring your emails in person.
3. Out of office: I have gone to remember what my own name sounds like without a meeting attached to it.
4. I'm away. Please send urgent matters to my manager, who will forward them to me, where they will die quietly.
5. I'm out of office until Tuesday. My inbox is being watched by the office plant, which has my full confidence.
6. I'm on holiday. This auto-reply is the hardest I have worked all year.
7. I'm away from my desk, but my out-of-office is doing the emotional labour of replying to you, so be kind to it.
8. Out of office: I'm currently unavailable, unreachable, and — for the first time since 2019 — unbothered.
9. I'm on leave. If your email is urgent, please reply to this message and wait. That is the whole plan.
10. I'm out of office. My laptop stayed home, my phone is on silent, and my out-of-office is the only one of us working.

## Picked

Candidate 1. Why: the "Future Me" twist is fresh (no beach/inbox cliché), the
laugh lands on the first read, and it still reads exactly like a real
auto-reply a person would switch on — it even answers the sender's real
question (who is handling my email?). Short (108 chars), every word earns
its place, no name or signature needed to make sense.
