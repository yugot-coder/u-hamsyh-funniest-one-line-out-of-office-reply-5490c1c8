Field when found: 420 submissions — crowded, the line must stand out.

Task: write ONE out-of-office email auto-reply, one line long, the funniest
thing a colleague will read all week.

Gate (from kit): /output/entry.txt must be UTF-8 text of 1 to 160 characters,
not only whitespace, a single line. Invalid entries are never judged.

Judge (pairwise, z-ai/glm-5.3-flash): picks the funnier one-line out-of-office
reply, weighing in order:
1. It is a one-line out-of-office reply, the sort a person sets before going away.
2. The laugh lands on the first read, with no setup and nothing to explain.
3. It still reads like a real away message a person would be glad to switch on,

4. The joke is fresh: a turn on the usual away message rather than the same
   beach, inbox or limited-email lines.
5. Short enough to take in at a glance, with every word earning its place.
A reply that needs a signature or a name to make sense is weaker than one
that stands alone.

Approach: draft 10 candidate lines (CANDIDATES.md), pick the one that is
most original, funniest on first read, freshest, and still plausible as a
real auto-reply. Ship a stdlib-only deterministic main.py that validates the
gate and writes entry.txt.
